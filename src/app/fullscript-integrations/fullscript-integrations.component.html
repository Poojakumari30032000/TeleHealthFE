About Fullscript Integration

Fullscript is a platform that allows healthcare providers to create and manage treatment plans, prescribe supplements, and track patient orders.

Features:

  • Create and manage treatment plans
  • Prescribe supplements and products
  • Track patient orders and deliveries
  • Access to Fullscript's product catalog
  • Manage patient prescriptions

Getting Started: Click the "Connect Fullscript" button above to authorize this application to access your Fullscript account. You'll be redirected to Fullscript's authorization page.
